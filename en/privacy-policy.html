Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Tirana Construction ("we", "our") respects the privacy of our website visitors and clients. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

2. Information we collect

When you fill out our contact or quote request form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, project address and the details you share about your project. We never collect financial or sensitive information through this form.

3. How we use your information

The information provided is used solely to respond to your quote request, contact you about your project and follow up on our services. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Sharing of information

Your information may be shared with subcontractors or suppliers directly involved in delivering your project, only to the extent necessary, and never for third-party marketing purposes.

5. Cookies

This site does not use advertising tracking cookies. Essential technical cookies required for the site to function properly may be used.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide against unauthorized access, loss or disclosure.

7. Contact us

For any question about this policy, or to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, write to us at info@tiranaconstruction.ca.
