Our story

Learn more about Tirana Construction

Tirana Construction was born from a passion for work done right, led by a carpenter originally from Tirana, Albania, who has called the South Shore of Montreal home for over fifteen years. What started as small renovation jobs between neighbours grew, over time, into a full team of contractors, designers and site managers.

Today we hold the same principle as on day one: where you live should make you happy. Every project, big or small, gets the same attention to detail.
